# Welcome to Orbitra

Orbitra is an **opinionated data platform** managed by **Átomo** that runs on your customers' cloud infrastructure. We've built Orbitra to deliver a world-class developer experience for data analysts, enabling them to focus on business applications without requiring a strong software engineering background.

## Designed for Data Analysts

Orbitra is purpose-built to empower data analysts:

- **Simple and intuitive** - Focus on your data, not infrastructure
- **Cloud-native** - Runs seamlessly on your customers' cloud
- **Managed by Átomo** - We handle the complexity so you don't have to
- **Production-ready** - Built on battle-tested open source technologies
